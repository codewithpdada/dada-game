Ludo Rules Guide

1. Starting Out: Needs a 6 to release pawn from yard.

2. Safe Stars: Star marked cells are safe from capture.

3. Extra Rolls: Rolling 6, capturing an enemy pawn, or reaching Home grants an extra roll.

4. Three 6s Penalty: Rolling three consecutive 6s forfeits the turn.

5. Exact Roll: Tokens need exact roll value to enter final center finish.
