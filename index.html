Ludo Rules Guide

1. Starting Out: A token can move out of the yard only if you roll a 6.

2. Extra Turns: Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent token grants an extra dice roll.

3. Capturing Tokens: Landing on an opponent token on an unprotected cell sends their token back to their yard.

4. Safe Spots: Cells marked with stars are safe zones where tokens cannot be captured.

5. Winning: First player to guide all 4 tokens into the central home path wins!
